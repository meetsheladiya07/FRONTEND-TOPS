About Me

Hello! My name is Meet, and I am a passionate food lover from Gujarat. I enjoy exploring different types of food and discovering the unique flavors, traditions, and stories behind every dish.

My Favorite Cuisine

My favorite cuisine is Gujarati cuisine because of its delicious flavors, traditional recipes, and healthy combination of sweet, spicy, and savory tastes. I especially love dishes like Gujarati thali, dhokla, fafda, undhiyu, thepla, and khichdi.

My Best Meal Experience

One of my best meal experiences was enjoying a traditional Gujarati thali with my family. The meal included a variety of dishes like dal, kadhi, vegetables, rotli, rice, farsan, sweets, and homemade pickles. Sharing this delicious food with family made the experience special and memorable.

My Favorite Dish - Surati Locho

Surati Locho is one of my favorite Gujarati street foods from Surat. It is a soft, spicy, and flavorful snack served with sev, onions, coriander, lemon juice, and chutney.
